2. Functional description

The AV-2029A is a first-year computer engineering unit at Purdue University, optimized for digital ASIC design with a growing focus on processor microarchitecture and GPU compute. The core pipeline already supports RTL design in SystemVerilog from specification through verification and synthesis, with an integrated RISC-V SoC as the flagship design. An on-chip verification subsystem drives pre-silicon validation through cocotb, Verilator, and the full LibreLane open-source EDA stack.

Currently ramping up a GPU hardware design track through Purdue SoCET, with long-term target of chip design at an ASIC company. When not at the EDA workstation, found either doomscrolling or bouldering.
