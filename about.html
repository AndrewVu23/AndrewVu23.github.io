2. Functional description

The AV-2029A is a first-year computer engineering unit at Purdue University, optimized for digital ASIC design with a growing focus on processor microarchitecture and GPU compute. The core pipeline already supports RTL design in SystemVerilog from specification through verification and synthesis, with an integrated RISC-V SoC as the flagship design. An on-chip verification subsystem drives pre-silicon validation through cocotb, Verilator, and the full LibreLane open-source EDA stack. The device additionally exposes a board-level interface for PCB design and prototyping in competitive robotics applications.

Currently ramping up a GPU hardware design track through Purdue SoCET, with long-term target of chip design at an ASIC company. Driven by a belief that understanding hardware from the transistor up gives you a perspective no amount of software abstraction can replace. When not at the EDA workstation, found competing in Robomaster, designing PCBs for combat robots, or writing about computer architecture.
