A little more

Origin. I was born and raised in Vietnam, then moved to the US for college. I picked Purdue because it’s a great engineering school, not because I had any clue what I wanted to do (lol). No regrets though. Two years in, I still don’t get tipping culture, which is why I’ve barely eaten out since my first semester.

Hardware. Everyone around me kept saying “get into semiconductors,” and I nodded along without really knowing what that meant. So freshman year I tried a bit of everything: PCB design, RFIC, analog. Over winter break I installed XSchem, fought with it for a week, and decided analog wasn’t for me (my Mac refusing to run it smoothly did not help). I picked up Harris & Harris DDCA instead and got hooked on digital design.

Motivation. Long term: land at a big tech company, get paid to do what I’d happily do for free, then use that money to travel. Maybe learn to tip without panicking. Short term: survive coursework while juggling too many side projects.
