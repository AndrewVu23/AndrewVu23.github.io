Origin

Born and raised in Vietnam, moved to the US for college. I picked Purdue because it is a good engineering school, not because I knew what I wanted to do. No regrets. Also, learning about US tip culture is why I have barely eaten out since my first semester.

Hardware

Everyone around me just said “get into semiconductor”, which I barely understood. Freshman year was a wander through board-level PCB design, RFIC, analog. Then over winter break I installed XSchem, tinkered for a week, and decided I would never touch analog again. Picked up Harris & Harris instead. The rest is history.

GPU Microarchitecture Research

accel-sim · cuda · nsight · ptx/sass  —  jul – aug 2026

•Restored PTX-mode simulation on all CUDA 11+ toolchains by fixing an Accel-Sim/GPGPU-Sim parser bug, and added an L2 write-hit counter comparable to NVIDIA’s profiler; both merged upstream

•Root-caused a SIGSEGV in the FP64 pipeline model (out-of-bounds index when a functional unit’s initiation interval exceeds its latency) and fixed a correlation bug that mis-attributed local-memory traffic, cutting L2 write error on one benchmark from -94.0% to +2.6%

•Investigated an unmodeled 4x over-count in L1-to-L2 write traffic; built a microbenchmark to confirm the merging mechanism; prototyped a merge buffer, reducing mean L2 write error from 19.48% to 2.40% and L2 write-hit error from 19.10% to 2.33% across 32 benchmarks, with 19 benchmarks bit-identical and worst collateral change -0.2%

32-bit RISC-V Pipelined Processor

systemverilog · computer architecture  —  feb – mar 2026

•Architected a 5-stage pipelined RV32I processor using Harvard memory architecture with a Hazard Control Unit; supported the complete base integer instruction set, achieving 100% architectural compliance via the RISCOF framework by co-simulating against Spike reference model

KV-PIMple

pim-aware kv-allocation for llm decode · hbm · pim · ai systems  —  sep 2026

•Discovered the DRAM row alignment that paged KV-cache allocation destroys for processing-in-memory (PIM) attention, raising simulated all-bank row-hit rate from 0.77 to 0.96, and modeled effective PIM bandwidth 2.3× at vLLM’s standard 16-token block by designing an allocator that fills one geometry-derived region

•Built a simulator of continuous-batching LLM decode, porting vLLM v0.2.7’s block allocator and covering four workloads: preempt-by-recompute, speculative decoding and MQA/GQA/MHA shapes; Verified with 85 tests including five validation gates (best/worst-case anchors, monotonicity, determinism, block conservation)

•Established the model’s credibility against two external cycle-level DRAM simulators by replaying its own block tables through Ramulator 2 and AttAcc’s all-bank PIM extension: placement agreed to 0.0023 mean row-hit across 9 samples on Ramulator, while timing is 1.4× more on AttAcc due to differences in policy assumption

the core

Through further exploring during winter break, I read DDCA by Harris & Harris cover to cover, then built the RV32I pipeline in February and pushed it through LibreLane to a signed-off Sky130 layout by March. This marks the first start to my chip design journey.

Apart from that, I also presented my research at the Spring Undergraduate Research Conference on my work at EPICS, which earned us the Presentation with Distinction award. This was my first ever research conference.
